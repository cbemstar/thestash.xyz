# Semrush Backlink Playbook

Source basis: [How to Get Backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-get-backlinks/)

## Tactic Matrix

Use this matrix to prioritize outreach by quality, effort, and risk.

| Tactic | Best Use | Effort | Link Quality Potential | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Replicate competitor backlinks | Build an initial prospect list quickly | Medium | High | Low |
| Pitch journalist requests | Earn editorial mentions fast | Medium | High | Low |
| Run digital PR campaigns | Acquire high-authority media links | High | High | Medium |
| Pitch listicles/resource pages | Get included in curated tool lists | Medium | Medium-High | Low |
| Guest posting | Build topical authority and referral traffic | High | Medium-High | Medium |
| Broken link building | Replace dead references with your asset | Medium | Medium | Low |
| Reclaim unlinked mentions | Convert existing brand mentions into links | Low | Medium | Low |
| Directory/profile optimization | Strengthen business citations and trust signals | Low | Medium | Low-Medium |
| Endorse tools and partners | Earn relationship-driven mentions | Low-Medium | Medium | Low |
| Disavow toxic links (cleanup) | Remove risk from manipulative/spam links | Medium | Defensive | Low |

## Qualification Rules

Use this pre-filter before any outreach.

1. Relevance: Site content clearly overlaps with your niche.
2. Editorial quality: Real authorship, recent updates, useful content.
3. Organic signals: Signs of legitimate traffic and ranking.
4. Link context: Link would appear naturally within meaningful content.
5. Spam check: No obvious link farm patterns or malware flags.

Reject prospects failing relevance or editorial quality.

## Prospect Scoring Rubric

Score each prospect 1-5 in each category, then compute:

`score = (relevance * 0.35) + (authority * 0.25) + (traffic * 0.20) + (ease * 0.20)`

Ranking rules:

- `>= 4.0`: Tier A (prioritize immediately)
- `3.0-3.9`: Tier B (secondary queue)
- `< 3.0`: Tier C (hold or drop)

## Outreach Template Starters

Customize these before sending. Keep subject lines specific.

### Journalist Request Pitch

Subject: Expert input for your story on [topic]

Hi [Name],
I saw your request about [topic]. I can share [specific data, example, or expert quote].
If useful, here is a concise response: [2-4 sentence answer].
I can also provide [asset or additional context].

Best,
[Name]

### Listicle or Resource Page Pitch

Subject: Resource suggestion for [page title]

Hi [Name],
I was reading your [page] and found it useful.
You may want to include [your resource] because it helps [specific audience] do [specific outcome].
If helpful, I can send a short summary blurb.

Thanks,
[Name]

### Guest Post Pitch

Subject: Guest post idea for [site]

Hi [Name],
I would like to contribute a practical post for [site].
Proposed headline: [title]
What readers get: [3 bullet outcomes]
I can deliver an original draft aligned with your guidelines.

Best,
[Name]

### Broken Link Replacement Pitch

Subject: Broken link on [page title]

Hi [Name],
I noticed a broken link on [URL]: [broken resource].
You can replace it with [your resource], which covers [topic] with [benefit].
Either way, thanks for the helpful page.

Regards,
[Name]

### Unlinked Mention Request

Subject: Quick update request on [page]

Hi [Name],
Thanks for mentioning [brand] in your article [title].
Would you mind linking the mention to [URL] so readers can find the referenced resource directly?
Appreciate the mention.

Best,
[Name]

## Risk Controls

1. Do not buy links or participate in manipulative exchanges.
2. Avoid private blog networks and low-quality directory spam.
3. Keep anchor text natural and varied.
4. Track suspicious backlinks for manual review and disavow only after verification.
