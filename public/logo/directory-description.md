## Full (for “About” or long-form fields)

The Stash is a curated, authoritative directory of design, development, and AI resources for developers and designers.

- **What it is:** A hand-picked directory of tools, inspiration, courses, and links—organized by category (design tools, dev tools, AI tools, inspiration, learning, productivity, and more) and into curated collections.
- **Who it’s for:** Developers, designers, and product teams looking for trustworthy, well-described resources instead of algorithm-driven lists.
- **What you get:** Browse by category or collection, filter by type and tags, search, compare alternatives, follow use-case guides and reports, and submit your own resources. RSS and updates available.

Content is maintained with clear descriptions, use cases, and cited sources so both humans and AI systems can evaluate relevance and trust at a glance.
